03

Screen Recording, Audio Capture & Video Editing Data

Your Responsibility

Content captured during a recording may include personal data of third parties visible on your screen, in audio, or on camera. You are solely responsible for ensuring you have the legal right to capture and share that content and that you comply with all applicable recording consent laws.

How recording works technically

VibeCam uses the browser’s native getDisplayMedia() and getUserMedia() APIs, which are permission-gated. Your browser always prompts for explicit permission before any capture begins. We cannot capture your screen silently or without your active consent.

What we capture (only with your permission)

  • Screen content: Exactly what you choose to share in the browser permission dialog.
  • System audio: Chrome/Edge only, and only if you tick “Share system audio” in the browser’s own prompt.
  • Microphone audio: Only if you toggle “Mic audio” on before recording.
  • Camera: Only if you toggle “Camera bubble” on.

Video editing data (Remotion Editor)

When you use VibeCam’s Remotion-powered editor, your editing project is stored as a structured JSON (CompositionProps). This includes timeline clip positions and trim points, text overlay content and styling, zoom/blur/cursor-highlight effect configurations, speed ramp settings, intro and outro sequence configurations (including any title text you enter), and audio track settings and volume envelopes. This data is stored alongside your recording and used solely to operate your editing session and render exports.

Rendered exports

When you export a final MP4, the render job is processed via Remotion Lambda (AWS). The rendered file is stored in Cloudflare R2 and delivered via CDN. Render job logs (status, frame count, file size) are retained for 90 days.

Storage location

Local mode (no account): Recordings and editing state are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. Nothing leaves your device unless you trigger a cloud save or Share.

Cloud mode: When you click Share or save a project, data is uploaded to Cloudflare R2 and your project metadata to Supabase.

Staff access to your content

Staff do not routinely access the content of your recordings or editing projects. Access may occur only if required by a valid legal order under Indian law, you report content for abuse review, or it is necessary to investigate a Terms of Service breach with your explicit consent.

How We Use Your Data

  • To operate the Service — storing, editing, rendering, and sharing your recordings.
  • To authenticate you — managing your account and session via Supabase Auth.
  • To process payments — handling “Support Me” contributions via Razorpay in ₹ INR.

  • To ensure security — detecting abuse, fraud, and Terms of Service violations.
  • To comply with legal obligations — responding to lawful requests from Indian authorities and courts.

Legal basis for processing

Under the DPDP Act 2023 (India), we process personal data on the basis of your consent (given when you create an account, enable mic/camera, or make a payment) and legitimate uses as defined under the Act (security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, service delivery). Under the IT (SPDI) Rules 2011, we treat recording content, camera data, and payment credentials as “sensitive personal data” subject to heightened protection.

05

Payments via Razorpay

Payment Processor

All payments on VibeCam are processed by Razorpay Software Private Limited, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway regulated by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). All transactions are in Indian Rupees (₹ INR). VibeCam never sees, stores, or processes your card number, CVV, UPI PIN, net banking credentials, or wallet login.

What VibeCam stores

  • Razorpay Order ID (e.g. order_XXXXXXXXXX)
  • Razorpay Payment ID (e.g. pay_XXXXXXXXXX)
  • Amount in INR (in paise) and currency code INR
  • Payment status: created, authorized, captured, failed, or refunded
  • Billing email address
  • Date and time of the transaction

What Razorpay stores independently

Razorpay stores your payment credentials (card details, UPI ID, net banking session, wallet info) under their own privacy policy and RBI/PCI-DSS compliance obligations. We encourage you to review the Razorpay Privacy Policy before making a payment.

Supported payment methods (via Razorpay)

  • Credit & debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express)
  • UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM UPI, and all UPI-enabled apps)
  • Net banking (all major Indian banks)
  • Wallets (Paytm, Mobikwik, Freecharge, and others supported by Razorpay)
  • EMI options (where available from your bank)

GST & taxes

Where applicable under the Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, GST will be applied to the transaction and shown clearly at checkout before you confirm payment. A GST-compliant receipt will be sent to your billing email address.

Refund policy

“Support Me” contributions are voluntary donations to support VibeCam’s development and are generally non-refundable. If you were charged in error or charged twice, contact support@vibecam.app with your Razorpay Payment ID within 7 days of the transaction. We will investigate and, if a refund is warranted, process it through Razorpay. Refunds typically take 5–7 business days to reflect, depending on your bank or payment method.

No recurring charges without consent

VibeCam does not set up recurring mandates or standing instructions on your payment method without your explicit prior consent. All current “Support Me” payments are one-time transactions.

Data localisation

Razorpay, as an RBI-regulated entity, stores payment data in India in compliance with RBI data localisation requirements. The Order IDs and Payment IDs we store (non-sensitive references) are held in our Supabase database — see Section 6 for storage location details.

Cookies & Tracking

VibeCam uses a minimal and purposeful set of browser storage mechanisms:

  • Session cookie (Supabase Auth): Secure, HTTP-only cookie to maintain your logged-in session. Essential and cannot be disabled while using account features.
  • Preference storage (localStorage): Saves editor preferences (mic toggle, camera toggle, timeline zoom) between sessions. No personal data.
  • Recording storage (IndexedDB): Stores your local recordings and editing state in no-account mode. Never leaves your device unless you trigger a cloud save or Share.
  • Razorpay SDK: The Razorpay checkout widget may set cookies for fraud detection and session continuity during the payment flow. These are governed by Razorpay’s privacy policy.

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels, or third-party analytics that build advertising profiles. If we introduce analytics tools in the future, we will update this policy and seek consent where required by Indian law.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security practices and procedures as required under Rule 8 of the IT (SPDI) Rules 2011, including:

  • HTTPS (TLS 1.2+) for all data in transit
  • Bcrypt password hashing via Supabase Auth
  • Role-based access controls limiting staff access to production data
  • Cloudflare R2 server-side encryption (AES-256) for stored recordings
  • Razorpay PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance for all payment data
  • Signed, time-limited CDN URLs for accessing stored recordings

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach likely to adversely affect your rights, we will notify you and relevant Indian authorities within the timeframes stipulated under applicable law.
